# TideKeeper

A browser game about guiding ships home through the fog.

**▶ Play it here: https://goodness371.github.io/TideKeeper/**

## How to play

Fog has rolled in over the skerries. Ships are making for your harbour, and they can only see the rocks while your beam is on them.

- **Aim the beam** at ships so they can see the rocks.
- **Watch the tide.** As it falls, hidden rocks break the surface.
- **A flickering red lantern** means a ship is about to hit something.

Three wrecks and the harbour board takes your keys.

Works on phone and computer. No download needed.
